## Eligibility Freshness Model

pair, its current pointer pair, and `reachability_epoch`. Validation resolves

epoch. Every proof, assessment, decision, AUTHORIZED/CERTIFIED state, and
Certification bound to the older epoch becomes advancement-ineligible even
before a stale Receipt is produced. No stale state can satisfy the atomic
predecessor read set.

The liveness proof is evidence for `OPEN_ELIGIBILITY`; it does not execute the
transition. A Transition is a finalized forward-only intent: it binds the
predecessor and reserved successor status, never a successor State, State CAS,
later marker, or later Receipt. The successor `MetaRepairStateV1` directly
binds that Transition pair.

For each non-root transition,
`ConstitutionalMetaRepairStateCASV1` then binds the finalized Transition,
predecessor pointer/State, successor State, exact reachability pointer/epoch,
and `committed_at`. One CAS persists the successor State/CAS, updates the
global pointer, and then permits
`ConstitutionalMetaRepairStateTransitionReceiptV1` from read-back. The Receipt
binds Transition, CAS, predecessor/successor, both current-pointer read-backs,
reachability read-back, result, and `committed_at`.

Transition idempotency binds contract version, kind, predecessor,
reserved-successor status, repair/baseline/target, reachability, authorizing
pair, and `transition_prepared_at`. CAS idempotency adds the exact successor
State and commit time. Retry resolves an existing identical Receipt. Same
idempotency different content fails closed. Crash before CAS leaves the
predecessor; after CAS the identical Receipt is reconstructed from committed
state. No transition can skip a state.

after Certification and before transaction preparation. The prepared DORMANT
successor State binds that intent; the root transaction and later marker bind
the State. Neither the Transition nor State binds the later marker, eliminating
a successor/predecessor identity cycle.

## Concurrency and One-Winner Model

All repair candidates contend on the single global MetaRepairCurrentPointer.
For a DORMANT predecessor, only one `OPEN_ELIGIBILITY` CAS can win. The winner
becomes the sole current repair identity. A losing candidate receives
`MetaRepairCandidateDispositionV1` with result `LOST_CONCURRENCY`, binding its
candidate identity, the winning ELIGIBLE state/Receipt pair, and exact
current-pointer read-back. It never becomes a MetaRepairState.

While ELIGIBLE, AUTHORIZED, or CERTIFIED, every different repair identity is
`INELIGIBLE_ACTIVE_REPAIR_EXISTS` and cannot contend at later stages. A moved
baseline or newer reachability epoch makes the current repair advancement-
ineligible immediately; `MARK_STALE` then terminalizes it. The new baseline
cannot open another repair until `RESET_DORMANT` installs its exact DORMANT
state.

Successful activation uses the same global pointer in its atomic read/write
set and writes DORMANT for the successor baseline. Therefore no separate
CERTIFIED state or loser survives the linearization point.
