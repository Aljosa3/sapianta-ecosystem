non-production invocation of the exact admitted public comparison function
against one ephemeral, completely authenticated Q V1 projection and one
independently authenticated current payload. Its only effect is an
observational `EQUAL`, `MISMATCH` or `FAILED_CLOSED` result.

It must remain zero-authority:


## C-D. Exact caller boundary

The sole allowed caller class for a future P9 step is:


registry dispatch, service, router, database trigger, state machine, Replay

## J-P. Comparison-result and disposal boundary

Only the immutable bounded result may be observed. Projection and current
payload content may not be returned, logged, cached, persisted or copied into
outcome evidence.

| Outcome | Exact future P9 handling |
|---|---|
| `EQUAL` | record only non-authoritative equality evidence; do not treat it as currentness authority; preserve and use the manual continuation |
| `MISMATCH` | reject the projection as a current-state substitute; retain manual continuation and fallbacks; request Human governance review if desired; do not repair or select |
| `FAILED_CLOSED` | accept no comparison claim; discard projection/result payload context; preserve committed evidence, manual continuation and fallbacks; do not retry automatically |

The complete in-memory result is discarded after the authorized observation.
Only a deliberately selected outcome-only governance evidence record may be
retained, and only when the P9 authorization explicitly permits it. That
record is non-authoritative and may contain only:

- exact authorization and admitted code/certification/admission identities;
- expected HEAD;
- contract identity and version;
- projection hash and authenticated-current hash, when returned;
- outcome and bounded failure-reason token;
- six zero-authority assertions;
- timeout/interruption disposition; and
- confirmation that manual/cognition/history fallbacks and no-mutation
  invariants remained true.

It must exclude projection bytes, current payload bytes, reconstructed source
content, absolute repository paths and secrets. Outcome evidence is a
governance artifact, not runtime persistence or constitutional state.
