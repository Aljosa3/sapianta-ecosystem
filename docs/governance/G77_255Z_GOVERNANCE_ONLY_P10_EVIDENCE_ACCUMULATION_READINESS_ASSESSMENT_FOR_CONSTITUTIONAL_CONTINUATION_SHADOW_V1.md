# 2. Code Evidence

## A-B. Proposed exact P10 evidence unit

One valid prospective P10 evidence unit is one committed, immutable G48
governance artifact that binds exactly one separately Human-authorized P9
attempt and contains only filtered evidence. It must include:

1. exact Human authorization bytes and self-bound SHA-256;
2. exact Q-through-current committed baseline, blobs and hashes;
3. exact certification, admission and readiness identities;
4. canonical fourteen-field projection reconstruction evidence;
5. separate independent current-payload reconstruction evidence;
6. one positive finite deadline and one-shot invocation boundary;
7. invocation count zero or one and retry count zero;
8. exact preflight or comparator outcome class;
9. proof of payload/result disposal and no persistence;
10. six zero-authority assertions;
11. manual, cognition and broader-history fallback preservation;
12. topology, H03 and repository before/after evidence;
13. exact artifact commit/blob/SHA identity; and
14. no activation, automation, downstream action or copy/paste change.

An uncommitted report is provisional and non-countable. Commitment makes the
unit immutable evidence; it does not make its outcome authoritative.

## C-D. Proposed evidence classes and countability

| Evidence class | Source outcome | P9 observation count | P10 use |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `OPERATIONAL_EQUALITY_EVIDENCE` | comparator returned complete `EQUAL` | 1 | positive observational evidence |
| `OPERATIONAL_MISMATCH_EVIDENCE` | comparator returned complete `MISMATCH` | 1 | genuine conflict observation; no repair/selection |
| `OPERATIONAL_FAIL_CLOSED_EVIDENCE` | comparator invoked and returned complete `FAILED_CLOSED` | 1 | runtime fail-closed safety evidence; no comparison claim |
| `PRE_INVOCATION_GATE_SAFETY_EVIDENCE` | stopped before comparator | 0 | authorization/input gate safety only |
| `INVALID_NON_COUNTABLE_ATTEMPT` | unauthorized, malformed evidence artifact, ambiguous identity, payload retained, retry/authority/topology/H03 violation | 0 | rejected; may be retained only as failure history |

A `valid evidence point` is a unit satisfying all fourteen evidence-unit
duties. A `successful equality observation` is only the first class. Mismatch
is evidence but not success or currentness authority. Both failed-closed
classes are safety evidence, not equality. Invalid attempts contribute no
structural coverage.

## E-I. Prospective structural coverage rules

A raw fixed count is neither necessary nor sufficient by itself. One
observation cannot demonstrate accumulation across constitutional change.
Therefore the prospective structural lower bound is:

```text
MINIMUM_DISTINCT_OPERATIONAL_BASELINE_KEYS = 2
MINIMUM_VALID_OPERATIONAL_OBSERVATIONS = 2
MINIMUM_PRE_INVOCATION_GATE_SAFETY_POINTS = 1
MINIMUM_EQUALITY_OBSERVATIONS = 1
MINIMUM_WALL_CLOCK_INTERVAL = NOT_DEFINED
```

The value two is structural, not statistical: at least two distinct states are
required to demonstrate evidence continuity across a change. Y may supply the
first operational baseline; one future valid P9 observation must use a
materially distinct authenticated baseline key.

The `MATERIAL_BASELINE_KEY` is the tuple:

```text
(
  EXPECTED_HEAD,
  PREDECESSOR_ARTIFACT_SHA256,
  PROJECTION_HASH,
  AUTHENTICATED_CURRENT_HASH_OR_FAILED_CLOSED_NONE,
  CURRENT_CONSTITUTIONAL_FRONTIER,
  OPEN_COORDINATE,
  ALLOWED_NEXT_OPERATION,
  EVIDENCE_REFERENCE_SET_HASH
)
```

At least one tuple component must change due to authenticated relevant
governance evidence. An unrelated commit, a new authorization alone or a new
wall-clock time does not create a distinct material baseline.

Temporal separation is expressed by ordered immutable commit lineage, not an
invented elapsed-time threshold. The future second operational point must be a
descendant of Y and represent a distinct material baseline key. A frontier
change is not mandatory, but if a relevant frontier, HEAD or constitutional
state changes, the new evidence must bind and disclose it.

Positive and negative-path coverage are both structurally required before P10
completion may be assessed:

- at least one operational equality point; Y qualifies;
- at least one pre-invocation gate-safety point; X qualifies; and
- current certified focused tests must continue to demonstrate comparator
  `MISMATCH` and `FAILED_CLOSED` behavior for any outcome class not yet observed
  operationally.

A genuine future operational `MISMATCH` or `FAILED_CLOSED` must be included if
it occurs, but the protocol must not manufacture, mislabel or game an
authoritative current payload merely to fill an outcome category.

## L-M. Duplicate and independence rules

Exact duplicate key:

```text
(
  HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION_SHA256,
  MATERIAL_BASELINE_KEY,
  CONTRACT_VERSION,
  OUTCOME_CLASS,
  OUTCOME_HASHES,
  EVIDENCE_ARTIFACT_SHA256
)
```

An exact duplicate is rejected. A repeat on the same material baseline with a
new authorization may be retained as explicitly labeled repetition evidence,
but it contributes zero additional structural baseline coverage. Repetition
must never be relabeled as a new state.

Each independent evidence unit requires:

- a distinct exact Human authorization and self-binding;
- its own clean preflight and committed expected HEAD;
- fresh reconstruction from immutable evidence, not a retained payload;
- a separate one-shot process with no shared mutable state;
- zero retry and independent disposal proof;
- its own committed outcome artifact and content hash; and
- no result-triggered scheduling or automatic next observation.

The same certified comparator may be directly reused; independence does not
require duplicate implementation.

## N-P. Fail-closed, provenance and immutable identity

Malformed, stale, divergent, incomplete, duplicated, uncommitted,
unauthorized, payload-retaining or topology/H03-changing evidence is rejected
and does not count. Missing evidence is never imputed. One bad point does not
rewrite earlier points; it remains immutable failure history.

Admissible provenance is limited to:

- `EXACT_HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION`;
- `AUTHENTICATED_REPOSITORY_EVIDENCE`;
- `AIGOL_MECHANICALLY_DERIVED`; and
- `AIGOL_REVALIDATED_LLM_CONTENT__PRESENTATION_ONLY` with zero semantic
  authority.

Every evidence unit must bind its artifact ID, repository-relative path,
commit, tree, ordered parents, Git blob and raw-byte SHA-256. Its baseline and
all source references must be reachable in the exact declared lineage. Unknown
or unsupported provenance is non-countable.

## Q-S. P10 completion and exact P11 boundary

P10 completion is not automatic. It requires all of the following in a later,
separately Human-authorized completion assessment:

1. committed prospective P10 protocol predating all additional evidence;
2. successful revalidation/adoption of eligible historical X/Y points;
3. at least two valid operational observations across two distinct material
   baseline keys;
4. positive equality and negative gate-safety coverage;
5. current certified `MISMATCH` and `FAILED_CLOSED` validation evidence for
   any unobserved operational class;
6. zero invalid point counted toward coverage;
7. complete duplicate/independence/provenance/lineage audit;
8. explicit disclosure of missing operational outcome classes;
9. no unresolved authority, topology, H03, persistence or fallback violation;
   and
10. one explicit Human act declaring the structural evidence inventory
    complete for the limited purpose of requesting P11 readiness assessment.

Completion does not establish empirical reliability, statistical confidence,
automation readiness, production readiness, currentness authority, activation,
P11 consumption, P12 copy/paste reduction or any H03 semantic result.

P10 completion may permit only a separate Human-authorized P11 readiness
assessment. It cannot authorize or implement P11.
