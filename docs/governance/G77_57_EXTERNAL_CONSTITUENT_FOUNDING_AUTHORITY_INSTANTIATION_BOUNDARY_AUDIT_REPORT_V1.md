## Exact Semantic Function of the External Authority

The external constituent authority performs one semantic function only: it
supplies the independently prior normative warrant and exact one-shot founding
act that the current Constitution cannot supply for its own first repair. It
does not implement the successor, operate SAPIANTA, classify ordinary G70
impact, or become a permanent owner.

| Function | Exact source or owner | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| decision source | existing Human Authority for the exact final `ADOPT_EXACT_TARGET` or `REFUSE_EXACT_TARGET` choice | Human selects content; external premise makes that choice eligible for the one founding domain; transport order has no authority |
| authority source | genuinely independently prior external constituent authority | supplies the non-self-derived constituent premise and one-shot Instrument; no internal role may substitute |
